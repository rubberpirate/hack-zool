# Z-Split

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

SvelteKit, Svelte 5, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, Motion, optimized SVGs.

## Users

Organizers paying event participants, contributors, or bounty winners; recipients redeeming a one-time claim.

## Product Purpose

Pay a room using shielded ZEC without collecting recipients' wallet addresses.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Seven reference screens plus a room overview, claim-link entry, product walkthrough, and plain-language privacy page. Complete organizer and recipient flows, mock funding and redemption, responsive layouts, reduced-motion support. The landing page explains the product with scroll reveals and draggable illustrated objects. This deliverable is a frontend demonstration, with no real transactions or backend integration.

## Brand Commitments

Reproduce all seven supplied wireframes: bold condensed headings, handwriting, lime highlights, outlined controls, playful vector artwork.

## Evidence on Hand

wireframe/ contains seven visual references. z-split-project-info.md supplies product context.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-accessible interactions, labeled controls, readable contrast, reduced-motion support.
